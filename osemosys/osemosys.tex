\begin{frame}{Energy System Models}
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item Models are simplified representations of reality
    \item Energy systems can be simplified and represented in organized model structures
    \item Energy system models aim to represent all components ans process in energy systems with mathematical equations
    \item This allows different future scenarios to be assessed
    \item Crucially, energy system models provide useful insights, but cannot predict the future with certainty.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{frame}
